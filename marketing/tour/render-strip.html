note - how these renders were produced

A widget-test harness (test/render/render_screen_test.dart); no app code touched. It pumps the real PolarisHomeRouteView, which initialises PolarisSDK itself in the demo session, and renders it at 390 logical px (dpr 3) at the full content height.

The data is the SDKs' own demo data. The test activates a demo session and the SDKs register their real repositories; base_sdk's DemoGatewayInterceptor answers them from polaris_sdk's and auth_sdk's own fixtures. The borrower is signed in through the real AuthRepositoryFacade.login.

Real fonts: Inter and Montserrat are the genuine files Google serves, committed under test/render/fonts/ and verified by google_fonts' own length and SHA-256 check. Remix and MaterialIcons come from the pub cache and the Flutter SDK cache.

what these frames deliberately do NOT show

Loan history is NOT seeded. The loans store is written by the device as applications are made, so a widget test starts empty and the frame shows the genuine fresh-device state.

The brand palette is not applied in this harness. Polaris has no applyAppBrandColors import wired into the harness yet, so colours are the AppStyle defaults.
